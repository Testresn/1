Bihyung is a character from the Webnovel and Webtoon Omnicient Reader's Viewpoint. He was the dokkaebi streamer on whose channel Kim Dokja featured for the beginning part of the scenarios. Due to Kim Dokja, his channel grew quickly and eventually he reached the Great Dokkaebi status.

"I can't reveal any information to you. Except that... Until the moment I breathe my last, I shall tell my story with you."

—Bihyung to Kim Dokja, Omnicient Reader's Viewpoint, Chapter 467

Appearance

At the start of the novel, Bihyung was the size of a soccer ball, but he grew as the story persisted.[5] In the webtoon as mid grade dokkaebi he has a more humanoid form with long ears and wears a black robe with gold highlights.

“His appearance had changed a lot since meeting the Dokkaebi King. He is now in a complete humanoid figure, with a white tiger leather draped over his shoulders and three yellow horns emanating the powers of Story on his head.”[6]

Personality

The Start of the Scenarios

He was a standard dokkaebi who didn't mind putting humans under extremes for a better story. He was outspoken, simple-minded, and very subservient to constellations.

Along the Scenarios

His personality changes through the influence of Kim Dokja and his companions. He is loyal, devoted and selfless.

History

He was the creator of the Random Item Box.[7] He was fired from the Star Stream Administration Bureau after the product was forced to be banned by the bureau and became a streamer. Bihyung was introduced in the first scenario at the subway to the Seoul Dome, gleefully tormenting the incarnations. There, he meets Kim Dokja who claims to be able to make him Dokkaebi King and with whom he makes a contract and gives access to the Dokkaebi Shop.

After Kim Dokja vists the Underworld he convinces Bihyung to put the 41st Shin Yoosung's soul into his dokkaebi egg.

At the constellation banquet Bihyung pulls Kim Dokja aside to protect him from the constellations and scold him for coming without a guide. He has now become an intermediate dokkaebi and gives a speech at the banquet showing off his dokkaebi egg.[9]

When Biyoo is kidnapped by the Wenny Man Bihyung goes to save her but is overpowered and has to be saved by Yoo Joonghyuk.[10] He then oversees the Demon King Selection along with Dokgak and is upset when Baram and the Dokkaebi Buraeu interfere with the scenario to try and make Kim Dokja lose.[11]

He sacrifices himself to stop the other Great Dokkaebis from interfering with an anti- <Kim Dokja's Company> scenario.

Bihyung bet on <Kim Dokja's Company> when all the Great Dokkaebi wanted them to perish, therefore betting his own existence to Kim Dokja.He states that Dokja had promised to make him a Dokkaebi King in the first scenario, and it had extended beyond that. Bihyung says that he would tell his story with Kim Dokja.[5]

Trivia

His name is likely a reference to "Lady Dohwa and Bachelor Bihyeong" which is the oldest documented story of a dokkaebi. In the webtoon he originally had white sclera however it later changed to black. His hobby is reading Star Stream forums.[12]
